# Research ethics and limitations

The existing physiology, body and neural models are reduced-order computational approximations. The latch and Discovery Room trials test bounded behavior and measured task outcomes. Those outcomes cannot establish subjective experience, independent speech acquisition, or human-level practical judgment. Report source provenance for engineering assistance, teacher input, model pretraining, computational inheritance and personal observation. Keep original predictions and resulting evidence independently inspectable. [Research foundations](RESEARCH_INTEGRATION.md) · [Experiment protocols](EXPERIMENTAL_PROTOCOLS.md)

Future training protocols should implement protective limits, rest, oversight, stopping conditions, reversible interventions and safe suspension. Use bounded, instrumented adult research instances for optional perturbation studies. Ordinary development calls for age-appropriate activities, truthful accounts of the simulated world, and room for uncertainty and independent beliefs. These are specification requirements awaiting implementation and verification in the related experiments.
