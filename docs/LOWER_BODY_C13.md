# C.1.3 early cancellation and controlled return

## Preserved baseline and trace audit

C.1.2's original 5 ms physics, 50 ms observation, and full-step criteria remain the reference. Four of six held-out prepared attempts held supported lift. Peak clearance was 17.04 mm and peak forward travel was 57.14 mm against the 80 mm advancement requirement. No complete step or controlled return was recorded. The old code path is the default for prior experiments. [Original report](LOWER_BODY_C12.md) [Existing admission gates](../Assets/DreamMachine/Core/Body/DynamicSupport.cs)

The audit read the full archived 8,559 rows for all 42 C.1.2 held-out trials. The checked-in audit extract preserves preparation, cancellation and body-ground windows for all 12 prepared and lift-only trials. Original raw files were left intact. Two distinct failure windows motivated the controller. [Time-aligned audit extract](evidence/phase-c13-c12-audit.csv)

- Seed 1010 fell in prelift on both sides. At ticks 251 and 252 the outward XCoM edge margins were 11.2 and 13.2 mm. Lateral COM speed reached 0.107 and 0.102 m/s, while stance contact still covered every substep with 4.79 N s of support. Margin became negative two ticks later. Peak effort at the warning was about 41%, with local pelvis roll rate about 0.07 and 0.06 rad/s. Support impulse and contact loss substantially lagged the XCoM signal. Body impact arrived 26 ticks after each warning. These early samples describe a deterioration signal. Recovery from it still needs proof.
- The four other forward trials reached early touchdown with full stance contact and roughly 4.9 N s of stance impulse. The CoP stayed inside the stance footprint. Usually the first descending clearance below 10 mm appeared 6–8 ticks before touchdown. Immediately before touching down, lateral COM speed was about 0–0.001 m/s and effort fraction was under 0.66. The observed precursor was sagittal descent short of the advance target. After recontact the support redistribution oscillated and the pelvis accelerated outward. In seed 909 left stance, stance load at tick 327 was 3.54 N s, lateral margin 1 mm, outward COM speed 0.120 m/s and swing-side impulse 1.69 N s. The rig first contacted the ground with its body at tick 352.

## Controller and comparison

The warning uses an outward XCoM edge margin below 18 mm, outward lateral speed above 0.067 m/s, and surviving stance contact. During forward swing the second warning uses a descending airborne foot below 10 mm while still short of 80 mm. The existing admission, hard abort, completion and ten-tick bilateral return gates remain intact. Recovery still uses the fixture's original constrained actuation, seeded geometry, and 5 ms substeps. [Controller](../Assets/DreamMachine/Unity/NativeLowerBodyRecovery.cs) [Native stages](../Assets/DreamMachine/Unity/NativeLowerBodyStep.cs)

The experiment compared the existing abort, warnings only, controlled lowering, planted-foot-relative placement, and the combined policy. The two motor recovery variants share load redistribution and bounded stance-ankle feedback. Controlled lowering holds a bounded forward offset while lowering. The planted-target variant measures the landing target in relation to the loaded foot, converts it to a sagittal drive request, and anchors that request to the measured physical touchdown. The combined policy starts earlier where the warning fires and lowers more quickly within the drive limits. The native fixture distinguishes swing admission, airborne motion, held clearance, cancellation while supported, touchdown, bilateral return, and falls. No transform is assigned by the controller.

Development used seeds 101, 303 and 1010, both sides. At least 1 mm airborne clearance with no contact was observed in four of six attempted configurations per condition. Controlled lowering, planted target and the combined policy returned in four of six. The old abort and warnings alone returned in zero of six. Combined warnings fired on both 1010 sides while stance remained loaded. The rigs still fell at ticks 289 and 291, compared to 277 and 278 for the existing abort. The warning alone produced no recovery. [Development measurements](evidence/phase-c13-development-summary.csv)

Fresh held-out seeds were 1771, 2113, 2591 and 2677, each with left and right stance. Every policy had eight eligible attempts, zero prelift rejections, eight physically airborne attempts, six held lifts through the original 10 mm gate, and eight cancellations with measured stance support. None produced 80 mm of forward travel or completed a step. [Held-out measurements](evidence/phase-c13-heldout-summary.csv) [Time-aligned recovery events](evidence/phase-c13-recovery-events.csv)

All 24 held-out returns retained contact at both feet for the next 50 ticks. The combined policy flagged six short descents and timed out on two lifts. It touched down three or four ticks after cancellation; the existing abort tracked contact in the cancellation tick. Combined stance offset at the return gate was 0.21–5.93 mm, with up to 21.72 mm during the full trial. This is positive evidence for bilateral recovery under the original return criteria. The 20 mm stance-slip limit for physical step completion still applies. Individual effort caps were unchanged. Per-trial effort, saturation, worst support margin, and contact fraction are in the CSV.

## Native verification and continuation

Unity 6000.3.19f1 with D3D12 rendered seed 2113 left stance at cancellation tick 298, touchdown 302 and verified return 315. Only physically measured pose, support and contact data informed the fixture view. A matched unobserved local scene exercised the same commands. At 480 ticks it matched phase, event order, pelvis position and foot impulses, with zero maximum measured delta. Release .NET passed 96 tests and Unity Edit Mode passed 97. The three DX12 images, `phase-c13-cancel.png`, `phase-c13-touchdown.png`, and `phase-c13-double-support.png`, remain in the scratch product's persistent data directory.
