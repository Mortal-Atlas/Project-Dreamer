# Phase C.1.1 · Lateral balance and sensorimotor support

## Prespecified protocol

Keep the original C.1 fixture as the baseline. Compare its ballast-only transfer to two variants with identical masses, collider geometry, rail, friction and sagittal torque caps. The frontal-plane variant adds bounded bilateral hip and ankle rolls. The feedback variant adds bounded changes based on the pelvis sensor and foot loading. Run development on seeds 101, 202 and 303. Reserve seeds 606, 707 and 808, including stage 3, for final comparison. Measure both sides from fresh centered trials. Use a separate 0.35 N·s lateral perturbation into the loaded side or away from it on the final seeds. Use the existing 0.05 s tick and ten 0.005 s PhysX steps.

At each target of 60%, 70%, 80%, 85%, and 90% stance loading, wait 60 ticks after the command and measure 40 consecutive ticks (2 s). A target passes only when at least 36 of the 40 individual readings and their arithmetic mean meet the target. Count a failed window as a failure and record the limiting conditions. Preserve C.1's geometry, slip, load-momentum and effort limits. For transfer, an unloaded swing foot may lose contact. The stance foot must produce positive impulse for the entire measured window. Retain zero non-foot ground contacts, neutral pelvis height ±25 mm, tilt ≤8°, knee angles in (0, 0.30] rad, joint overshoot ≤0.025 rad, penetration ≤8 mm, stance drift ≤20 mm, ≤5% actuator-cap use, and RMS momentum residual ≤0.15 N·s. Retain C.1's six-second 95% bilateral criterion for neutral standing. Check CoP against the contact impulse sum and projected foot bounds before calling it available.

For the physical swing, keep the 85% threshold and additionally require the most recent 20 ticks of valid support to meet it consecutively. Attempt at most one swing in a trial, only after the gated transfer. Success requires at least 10 mm positive clearance, 80 mm actual forward travel, physical recontact, continued stance contact and ≤20 mm stance drift through the swing. Require ten subsequent ticks of bilateral support. Report incomplete transfer, aborted swing and fall separately. A test of the gate or an analytical trajectory cannot count as a native swing result.

Record measured and commanded angles and effort, contact impulse and CoP validity, pelvis and mass state, stance and action states, and sensor tick. Publish the difference between stepping commands and measured outcomes. Keep original and new results separate.
