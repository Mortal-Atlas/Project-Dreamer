# Phase C.1.1 · Lateral balance and sensorimotor support

## Prespecified protocol

Keep the original C.1 fixture as the baseline. Compare its ballast-only transfer to two variants with identical masses, collider geometry, rail, friction and sagittal torque caps. The frontal-plane variant adds bounded bilateral hip and ankle rolls. The feedback variant adds bounded changes based on the pelvis sensor and foot loading. Run development on seeds 101, 202 and 303. Reserve seeds 606, 707 and 808, including stage 3, for final comparison. Measure both sides from fresh centered trials. Use a separate 0.35 N·s lateral perturbation into the loaded side or away from it on the final seeds. Use the existing 0.05 s tick and ten 0.005 s PhysX steps.

At each target of 60%, 70%, 80%, 85%, and 90% stance loading, wait 60 ticks after the command and measure 40 consecutive ticks (2 s). A target passes only when at least 36 of the 40 individual readings and their arithmetic mean meet the target. Count a failed window as a failure and record the limiting conditions. Preserve C.1's geometry, slip, load-momentum and effort limits. For transfer, an unloaded swing foot may lose contact. The stance foot must produce positive impulse for the entire measured window. Retain zero non-foot ground contacts, neutral pelvis height ±25 mm, tilt ≤8°, knee angles in (0, 0.30] rad, joint overshoot ≤0.025 rad, penetration ≤8 mm, stance drift ≤20 mm, ≤5% actuator-cap use, and RMS momentum residual ≤0.15 N·s. Retain C.1's six-second 95% bilateral criterion for neutral standing. Check CoP against the contact impulse sum and projected foot bounds before calling it available.

For the physical swing, keep the 85% threshold and additionally require the most recent 20 ticks of valid support to meet it consecutively. Attempt at most one swing in a trial, only after the gated transfer. Success requires at least 10 mm positive clearance, 80 mm actual forward travel, physical recontact, continued stance contact and ≤20 mm stance drift through the swing. Require ten subsequent ticks of bilateral support. Report incomplete transfer, aborted swing and fall separately. A test of the gate or an analytical trajectory cannot count as a native swing result.

Record measured and commanded angles and effort, contact impulse and CoP validity, pelvis and mass state, stance and action states, and sensor tick. Publish the difference between stepping commands and measured outcomes. Keep original and new results separate.

The development pilot exposed a trial-design issue after its first high-target collapse. Later targets were inheriting the fallen state. The final protocol uses a fresh centered trial for every target. Transfer trials start with 180 neutral ticks. The standing reference uses 40 settling ticks and 120 sampled ticks. The force-plate reference informs sensor interpretation and units. The numeric acceptance gates above are fixed before held-out evaluation. [External sources](EXTERNAL_RESEARCH_RESOURCES.md)

## Implementation

`Sagittal` keeps the six original hinges and sliding ballast. Both new variants use native anchored two-axis PhysX joints in the hips and ankles, with local sagittal +X rotation and frontal +Z roll. Joint translation and yaw are locked. The knees retain the original hinges. All masses, seeded collider dimensions, gravity and sagittal torque caps come from C.1. Hip roll is limited to ±0.30 rad and 16 N·m. Ankle roll is limited to ±0.27 rad and 12 N·m. Both use a 220 N·m/rad spring and 9 N·m·s/rad damping, evaluated with projected two-body inertia. Applied torques act in equal-and-opposite pairs. No world-frame support force, root lock, or position warp is used.

`FrontalPose` ramps the opposing hip and ankle roll targets toward the selected direction. The final hip reference is at most ±0.075 rad, with setpoint motion of 0.002 rad per tick. `SensorFeedback` adds a capped low-pass load correction to the roll reference and caps ankle tilt and gyro correction at ±0.045 rad. Both variants have the same physical lateral effort caps. The original pitch controller still uses laboratory COM and world gravity as documented in C.1. The new feedback loop reads the previous tick's simulated pelvis gravity and gyro and normalized sole impulse. COM, support envelopes and momentum remain laboratory observations. CoP is also diagnostic in this controller. The support and swing interlock uses explicit privileged measurements of geometry, drift and contact. None of these signals are wired into a household brain or memory library.

The pelvis sensor samples orientation, gravity transformed into body coordinates, gyro, and body-frame kinematic acceleration from the successive velocity samples. The calibrated trials use zero noise. Optional reproducible bias-like noise affects gravity on body X only and advertises its bound up to 0.06 m/s². The current ideal orientation and proprioception simplify estimation. Sole readings report contact transitions, impulses, shoe clearance and drift. For the two extended variants, CoP weights PhysX contact positions by their vertical impulse and checks its impulse sum and foot bounds. A rejected CoP is empty in the export. Phase C.1 sagittal control retains its original inexpensive callback and reports CoP unavailable. [Unity contact impulse API](https://docs.unity3d.com/6000.3/Documentation/ScriptReference/ContactPairPoint-impulse.html)

The first swing uses bounded sagittal joint setpoints generated from a body-relative forward/clearance trajectory. A swing can start only after neutral standing and sustained stance verification. Real contact determines the outcome. Loss of stance contact ends the commanded motion and requests centered support. The experiment then observes another two seconds, retaining the first physical fall event regardless of the swing outcome.

## Results

The first development candidate used 0.16 rad of hip reference per full command and reacted quickly to load error. Standing was stable. Above the 60% target it fell, and several load readings of 100% came from a foot still touching while the body rested on the floor. The smaller, slower ramp produced six passing 85% development windows with the pose controller and none with sensor feedback. [Initial pilot](evidence/phase-c11-initial-roll.csv) · [Development summary](evidence/phase-c11-development-summary.csv)

The held-out comparison used two sides × three fresh seeds for each controller. All 18 centered standing windows passed. The numbers below count complete target passes, including the physical acceptance checks. [Per-trial held-out data](evidence/phase-c11-heldout-summary.csv)

| Controller | 60% | 70% | 80% | 85% | 90% |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Sagittal ballast | 0/6 | 0/6 | 0/6 | 0/6 | 0/6 |
| Frontal pose | 6/6 | 6/6 | 6/6 | 6/6 | 0/6 |
| Frontal with sensor feedback | 6/6 | 6/6 | 6/6 | 0/6 | 0/6 |

At the 85% target, frontal pose produced mean stance loads of **86.33–88.36%**. Every passing window met the load for all 40 samples. Peak stance drift was 0.074 mm and peak pelvis tilt 0.237°. Sagittal control measured 60.72–65.41% for the same command. Two sensor-feedback trials near 85% lacked sustained loading and the other four made non-foot ground contact. The 90% pose trials also failed. No 90% window passed. The 18 small held-out lateral taps at the 70% command produced no non-foot contacts and at most 0.205° pelvis tilt. These taps do not establish the limits of the lateral reflexes. [Perturbation samples](evidence/phase-c11-perturbations.csv)

**Six physical swings started and all six aborted at tick 296, 0.80 s after the request, when the stance foot lost contact.** Swing-foot clearance reached 1.12–4.51 mm. Forward travel reached at most 1.64 mm. The fixed limits were 10 mm and 80 mm, along with stance continuity and stable recontact. Five rigs later made non-foot ground contact within the two-second follow-up. The sixth had no body impact and still ended without bilateral support. ## Observatory and next experiment

The isolated **Dream Machine → Start Lower-Body Laboratory** tray defaults to frontal pose with an 85% target. It can cycle between controllers or targets, transfer either way and request one gated swing. It shows separate vestibular, load, CoP, clearance, pitch/roll effort, gate and fall readings. The axes, reaction markers and support envelope follow the actual physical body. F6 toggles the tray. CSV schema 2 identifies each tick as an isolated firsthand physical measurement, with engineered commands recorded in their own fields. It includes controller, seed, fixed step, sensor state, privileged data and action or fall outcomes. Imported measurements remain in the external resource registry, with no lab evidence inserted into lived memories. [Unity Play Mode screenshot](evidence/phase-c11-observatory.png)

Release .NET passed 70 tests and Unity Edit Mode passed 71. The isolated DX12 harness verified body-relative sensor coordinates, noise bounds, tick alignment, actuator caps, scene isolation and the swing interlock. The 200-sample observed 4× run matched unobserved single stepping exactly for pelvis positions and both foot-impulse vectors. The tray verified a paused start, exactly one step, resume and pause, fixture selection and reset, and an actual 281-row export. Household control and observed runs each restored their checkpoint at tick 1,140 and produced the same SHA-256 `13ffff7d9d089b331554ad08adae22171ababf3cb56bf45c3f8dd7565dee562d`. The original clock smoke needed an explicit starting tray state because the household now defaults to an open tray. The family pose and layout smoke passed separately. [Reproduction commands](../tools/unity/README.md) · [Separate native and household benchmarks](PERFORMANCE_BENCHMARKS.md#phase-c11-lateral-balance)

The next experiment should keep the gait interlock and add an explicit prelift interval with stance pressure and predicted motion inside the planted footprint. Compare a slow stance-ankle stabilization ramp and a delayed swing hip target on new seeds. Measure the reaction transfer through the entire swing and retain the same 85% starting load, 10 mm clearance, 80 mm travel and 20 mm stance-drift criteria. Recovery, whole-body balance and learned locomotion remain open. Visual mesh work has a separate [retargeting compatibility table](ASSET_AND_LICENSE_REGISTRY.md#phase-c11-lower-body-retargeting-compatibility).
