# Project vision and development roadmap

Project Dreamer tests whether practical wisdom can develop through embodied practice, curiosity, education, social interaction, constructive imagination and reflection. Dreaming Machines and the Ten Lens Ontological Perspective supply the organizing research questions. The waking O2/O3 observer processes, nighttime O5 consolidation and Level 10 possibility space are experimental ideas whose effects can be measured. Artificial phronesis is a long-term research objective. See the [current implementation](ARCHITECTURE.md) and [protocols](EXPERIMENTAL_PROTOCOLS.md).

## Subsystem design boundaries

- **Curiosity engine and developmental learning.** A learner's observations, action history and own predictions supply uncertainty, novelty and prediction progress. A bounded statistic by context/action can use a short and long loss window, a finite novelty allowance, energy/fatigue cost and revisit limits. Safety gates are enforced by the environment outside scoring. Begin with perceptual and mechanical questions. Social, intellectual, creative, reflective and existential question generation need task definitions and separate validity checks. Persistent interests, open curricula and capability-based stages follow evidence from the first experiment.
- **Biological bootstrap.** Use a release/checksum manifest plus stable external neuron IDs, directed aggregated edges with contact counts, regions, cell types and transmitter evidence. Preserve unknowns. Start from synthetic fixtures and an approved circuit subset. Keep immutable topology shared and synaptic learning state private. Evaluate recurrent strongly connected components, weighted hubs, reachability from declared sensory to motor nodes, community partitions, and signed-edge sensitivity. Use deterministic seed-controlled extraction and controls. See [sizes and cost estimates](PERFORMANCE_BENCHMARKS.md#connectome-feasibility).
- **Functional neural system and bridge.** Study attention, working memory, action selection, motor correction, sensory integration and neuromodulation as testable functional populations. Use the structured silicon/reasoning and experiential wetware sides as proposed engineering analogies from Dreaming Machines. Keep a deterministic integrator of observable context and competing objectives. Bridge messages should carry direction, sender, tick, payload type, provenance, confidence and sequence; queues should enforce capacity, per-direction latency, disconnect/drop policy and recovery with explicit resynchronization. Experimental comparisons include full, restricted, delayed and disconnected conditions. Quantum linkage remains speculative.
- **Three libraries and continuity.** Library I carries priors and knowledge with origin labels. Library II generates possibilities and reproducible coordinates with finite representation. Library III stores evidence and lived history by append, including contradictory evidence and superseding conclusions. A typed record links identity, event tick, source kind, observations and belief revisions. Preserve distinctions among direct observation, teacher statement, inference, memory, hypothesis, imagination and dream. Track identity through checkpoints with schema versions, world data and random state.
- **Constructive imagination and dreaming.** A mental scene graph owns stable object and component IDs, transforms, geometry parameters, materials, relationships and version history. Prototype five legs, six wings, three eyes and two mouths with independently editable components. A replay links to observed events, logs a counterfactual and queues a waking test. Evidence updates follow waking feedback. Generative assistance can later work through explicit scene-graph edits and constraints.
- **Neural Observatory.** Start with an event inspector driven by actual kernel messages. Compare logged and displayed events and verify equal simulation output with the view disabled. Later render a translucent brain with selectable regions, bridge traffic, adjustable layers, replay, camera controls and data-linked population activity. Label synthetic previews and recorded datasets. Philosophical overlays describe the intended computational roles. See the [available data today](ARCHITECTURE.md#inventory-and-gaps).

## Ethics and evidence

Use transparent conditions, provenance, bounded workload, voluntary rest, safe shutdown and human oversight. Keep scenarios benign and document permission for data and asset use. Treat simulated drives and affect as computational variables. Evidence for phronesis requires independently evaluated behavior in unfamiliar contexts with competing needs, changing circumstances and long-term consequences. A convincing verbal explanation supplies a separate measure from behavior. Reassess workload and distress assumptions if credible evidence of subjective experience emerges.
