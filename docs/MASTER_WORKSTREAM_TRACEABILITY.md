## Integration decisions after H3

The immediate reuse path is the same contact and actuator contract for one persistent held object, then a supported move and place with stable object state. The native fixture accepts the test object's geometry and disturbance from the evaluator. The grip policy sees pad force, duration and relative motion. Preserve that boundary when a household object index replaces the isolated trial setup. A command creates a live physical attempt and an evidence record owns the outcome. The household save owns settled identity and world state. The renderer follows that state.

A chair requires a free body and grips, verified pulling/rotation, a physical sit-down trajectory and measured seat load. Inertia, attention shifts and competing goals should produce genuine consequences. Commit those consequences to the world with the relevant source rather than resetting furniture or scripting accidents. Extend the contract to doors, drawers, lids, tools and artistic materials after supported manipulation and transport pass. Alice needs a separate calibration and acquisition ledger. The H2 learner API keeps subject identity attached to evidence. There is no H3 transfer of Arthur's parameters to Alice.

Reuse H2 firsthand records for Library III. Add a Library I affordance projection and Library II candidate/evaluation data only when grounded evidence or actual candidate construction exists. This leaves the Library II Babel construction and Library III browsable-history work visible without inventing records. [H2 attempt ownership](../Assets/DreamMachine/Core/Sim/HouseholdGripSession.cs) [H3 sensor contract](../Assets/DreamMachine/Core/Body/ContactGatedGrip.cs)

## Integration decision after H4

Use the verified support, transport and release path for one settled household object with a stable ID and explicit shoulder and arm support. Compare fixed and force-only policies first. The tested geometry proposals produced real repositioning with identical completion to their ablation, so a broader geometry search remains experimental. Persist the placement consequence and Library III reference in the household transaction. Keep Alice's H4 research skills, records and seed independent as her household bridge is developed. Mid-contact restoration, autonomous balance and physical clothing remain separate dependencies. [H4 integration and continuation](PHYSICAL_PLACEMENT_H4.md#unity-workflow-and-remaining-work)

## Integration decision after H5

Use the same world-owned ball and two destination regions to evaluate supported articulated shoulder and elbow control. Before extending interactions, measure anatomical reach, arm–torso clearance, carry stability and propagation of observed target error. Keep actual position, learned sighting and researcher goal separate. Work toward grounded movement through the room to recover failed or abandoned objects, with posture support and independent locomotion as prerequisites. [H5 dependency and verification path](HOUSEHOLD_TRANSFER_H5.md#unity-workflow-and-dependencies)

## Integration decision after H6

H6 articulation reaches and transfers weight through the persistent Ball, with release drift exposing the next control dependency. Add live visible Ball, hand and support estimation to the proprioceptive loop, compare deceleration under fixed H5 acceptance gates and retain actual failed consequences. Keep dynamic torso support, full-body balance and locomotion as the next integration boundary for self-directed pickup and recovery. [H6 transfer and persistence evidence](ARTICULATED_UPPER_BODY_H6.md#loaded-transfer-and-persistence)
